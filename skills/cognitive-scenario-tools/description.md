# Cognitive Scenario Tools

## Purpose

Use this module when a defined problem belongs to a concrete human, social, or
communication scenario and general analysis is not enough. It provides
situational lenses for relationship, negotiation, cooperation, shared resources,
competition, personal growth, and public expression.

## Applicable Scenarios

- relationship and communication ambiguity
- negotiation or cooperation with mixed incentives
- shared-resource or organizational coordination
- competition against stronger constraints or actors
- personal growth bottlenecks
- public expression, persuasion, or positioning

## Non-Goals

- Do not use game theory as proof that people are hostile.
- Do not turn weak signals into certainty.
- Do not use scenario labels to avoid difficult action.
- Do not try to control outcomes outside the user's influence.

## Usage Notes

For scenario analysis, always separate facts, interpretations, emotions, and
desired actions. End with a small verifiable action rather than an imagined
certainty.
