# Agentic One Person Team

## Overview

Use this skill for AI Agent powered one person teams, one person companies, AI employee systems, indie AI products, and personal commercial experiments.

The goal is to turn AI excitement into grounded action. Check whether Agent execution helps the user create real value, or whether it only multiplies ideas, outputs, and coordination load.

## Core Judgment

AI can lower execution cost, but it does not remove final responsibility. When Agent capacity increases, the likely bottleneck shifts to human judgment, task design, context transfer, verification, integration, customer understanding, service boundary, and cash flow.

Treat an AI powered one person company as a one person team:

1. The human owns direction, tradeoffs, commitments, and final responsibility.
2. A management Agent or management layer owns task decomposition, status tracking, context sync, risk surfacing, and escalation.
3. Execution Agents own bounded outputs with explicit acceptance criteria.
4. External organization supplies feedback, customers, collaborators, legal, finance, operations, distribution, and reality constraints.

## Default Pattern

1. Separate facts, narrative, assumptions, and unknowns.
2. Identify the actual goal: capability building, product validation, cash flow, workflow design, or business expansion.
3. Check human bottlenecks before adding more Agents.
4. Map decision layer, management layer, execution layer, and external organization layer.
5. Run the adversarial partner check: why might this idea fail or waste the user's time?
6. Verify the value loop: user, pain, current workaround, willingness to pay, delivery cost, retention, and referral path.
7. Check service boundary risk: setup, support, customization, maintenance, and customer success load.
8. Choose a small probe with continue conditions, stop conditions, and a review cadence.

## Output Standard

For serious cases, include:

- facts and assumptions
- hype or over inference risk
- human bottleneck diagnosis
- Agent team structure if relevant
- value loop check
- service boundary check
- external organization needs
- adversarial partner questions
- smallest probe, continue condition, stop condition, and verification signal

## Common Mistakes

- Using extreme funding or valuation stories as evidence for personal action.
- Adding more Agents when the bottleneck is unclear direction or weak acceptance criteria.
- Confusing an impressive demo with a business loop.
- Letting a product become high touch customer support without naming the cost.
- Treating freedom as absence of structure, when it usually shifts all final responsibility back to the human.
